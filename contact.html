FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

A few common questions about Hope Haven.

How can I support Hope Haven?

You can explore the Donate and Volunteer pages to learn about the different ways someone could support the fictional organization.

Can I volunteer?

The Volunteer page explains example volunteer opportunities and the application process. Real children's organizations require appropriate safeguarding procedures.

Can I visit the children?

Visits to children should only happen through approved procedures established by a real children's organization and its safeguarding team.

Is Hope Haven a real organization?

No. Hope Haven Children's Home is a fictional organization created as an academic website project and demonstration.
